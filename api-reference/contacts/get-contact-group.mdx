---
title: "Get a contact group"
description: "Retrieve one contact group by ID."
api: "GET /contact_groups/{contact_group_id}"
public: true
---

<ParamField header="access_token" type="string" required>API access token.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="contact_group_id" type="integer" required>Contact group ID.</ParamField>

Returns the group name, creator, contact count, and timestamps. Returns `404` when the group does not exist.
