---
title: "List contact groups"
description: "Retrieve contact groups and their contact counts."
api: "GET /contact_groups"
public: true
---

<ParamField header="access_token" type="string" required>API access token.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="limit" type="integer">Maximum number of groups to return.</ParamField>
<ParamField query="cursor" type="string">Pagination cursor.</ParamField>

Each group includes its ID, name, creator, contact count, and ISO 8601 timestamps.
