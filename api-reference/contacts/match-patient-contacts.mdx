---
title: "Match patient contacts"
description: "Find patient contacts that may match data extracted from a fax."
api: "POST /patient_contacts/matches"
public: true
---

<ParamField header="access_token" type="string" required>API access token.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="fax_uuid" type="string" required>UUID of the fax containing the patient data.</ParamField>

```json
{"fax_uuid":"f2f4c4f6-9d31-4b68-a901-c0f5f1d06dbe"}
```

Matching considers first and last name, date of birth, email, phone, physician name, medical record number, and gender. Gender contributes to the match only when the first name also matches.
