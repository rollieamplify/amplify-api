---
title: "List automations"
description: "List the automations configured in your workspace."
openapi: "GET /v1/automations/list"
---

Returns the automations configured in your workspace, including their triggers, status, execution order, and pagination metadata.

The `id` returned for each automation is used by [Get automation details](/api-reference/customer-v1/fax-automations/post-automations-detail.mdx).
