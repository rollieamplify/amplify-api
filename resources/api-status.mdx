<Note>
  The status page covers every Amplify service in a single view: the **REST API**, **webhooks**, **dashboard**, **fax delivery**, and **messaging**. Check it first whenever you experience unexpected errors or degraded response times.
</Note>

## What to Do During an Incident

If your API requests are failing or behaving unexpectedly, follow these steps before opening a support ticket:

1. **Check [status.amplify.xyz](https://status.amplify.xyz)** for any active incidents or degraded components that match what you're experiencing. The page updates within minutes of a new incident being detected.
2. **Subscribe to incident notifications** on the status page by clicking **Subscribe to Updates** and choosing your preferred channel (email, SMS, or RSS). You'll receive automatic updates as the incident progresses to resolution.
3. **Contact support at [support@amplify.xyz](mailto:support@amplify.xyz)** if you cannot find a matching incident or if your issue persists after a listed incident is resolved. Include your account ID, the affected API endpoint, example request/response pairs, and a timestamp range to help the team triage quickly.

<Info>
  When contacting support, attach any relevant request IDs from the `X-Request-Id` response header. This header is present on every Amplify API response and allows the support team to locate your specific requests in our logs immediately.
</Info>

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Amplify's uptime commitments vary by plan. Review your plan details in the [Amplify dashboard](https://app.amplify.xyz) under **Settings → Plan** for the exact SLA that applies to your account, including monthly uptime targets, credit eligibility, and the process for filing an SLA credit request.

<Accordion title="General SLA guidance">
  While specific uptime percentages are defined per plan, the following principles apply across all paid Amplify plans:

  - **Scheduled maintenance** is announced at least 48 hours in advance on the status page and does not count against your uptime SLA.
  - **SLA credits** are calculated based on the cumulative downtime in a given calendar month. Log in to your dashboard to submit a credit request within 30 days of the affected month.
  - **Beta features** (such as the Forms API) are excluded from SLA commitments and may experience higher variability. They are clearly labeled `beta` in the API reference.
</Accordion>
